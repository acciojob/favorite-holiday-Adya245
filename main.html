Manali


Manali is a beautiful hill station located in Himachal Pradesh, India. It is famous for its snow-capped mountains, scenic valleys, adventure activities, and pleasant weather throughout the year.

    Top 5 Attractions

  1. Solang Valley – Famous for adventure sports and stunning views.
  2. Rohtang Pass – Known for its breathtaking snowy landscapes.
  3. Hadimba Temple – An ancient temple surrounded by cedar forests.
  4. Old Manali – Popular for its cafes and vibrant culture.
  5. Jogini Waterfall – A beautiful waterfall and trekking spot.

    Must-Try Foods

  • Siddu – A traditional Himachali bread.
  • Thukpa – A delicious noodle soup.
  • Madra – A famous Himachali curry.
  • Momos – Popular steamed dumplings.
  • Babru– A Himachali version of stuffed kachori.
